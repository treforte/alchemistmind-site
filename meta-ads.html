Common problems we fix

Where law firms lose money on Meta Ads.

Instant forms full of junk

Low-friction lead forms producing fake numbers and people who never answer.

No Conversions API

Browser pixels alone miss conversions, so Meta's optimization runs on partial data.

Creative fatigue

The same few ads running for months while frequency climbs and cost per case drifts up.

What we do

Optimized on quality, not volume.

We set up Meta to learn from signed cases, then build creative that earns attention in the feed without crossing bar advertising lines.

  • Conversions API + CRM feedback

    Server-side tracking and signed-case events sent back so Meta optimizes on quality.

  • Creative testing system

    Video, static and testimonial-style creative tested on a regular cadence.

  • Lead quality filters

    Form design and qualifying questions that trade a little volume for far better leads.

  • Landing pages built for Meta traffic

    Mobile-first pages and forms designed for scrollers, not searchers, and tested against cost per signed case.

Yes, especially for estate planning, PI awareness and mass tort. They work best when Meta receives signed-case data via Conversions API.
