The problem

Your ads report leads. Your partners care about signed cases.

Most law firms spending serious money on ads can tell you their cost per lead. Almost none can tell you which channel, campaign or keyword produced last month's signed cases. That gap is where budget disappears.

01

No cost per signed case

You know what a lead costs. You don't know which leads signed, so you can't tell a $300 lead that signs from one that never answers.

Budget follows volume, not revenue
02

Google is optimizing for the wrong thing

Smart bidding chases form fills and calls. Without signed-case data flowing back, the algorithm gets better at finding cheap, unqualified leads.

More leads, same caseload
03

A CRM nobody trusts

Duplicate contacts, missing sources and agency decks that never match intake. Every meeting starts with whose number is right.

Decisions made on gut feel
04

Intake runs on manual work

Clipboards, re-keying and unreviewed calls. Leads go cold and you can't tell if a bad month is marketing or intake.

Paid leads leak out the back door

FAQ

Questions firms ask us

What does Alchemist Mind Pros actually do?

We're a performance marketing agency for law firms. We manage paid media (Google Ads, Meta, Reddit and ChatGPT ads) and build the landing pages behind it, connect everything to your CRM with ClearSignal so you can see cost per qualified lead and cost per signed case by channel, clean up and automate HubSpot, and offer intake tools (AI Sales Coach and the Check-In App) to help you convert more of the leads you pay for.

What budget do you need?

At least $10K a month in ad spend, with the comfort to scale when results prove out. Most of our best results come from firms investing $100K+ a month, and we manage accounts spending more than $2M a month.

Do I have to use ClearSignal to work with you on PPC?

No. Many clients start with PPC management and add ClearSignal later. Our PPC is strongest when signed-case data flows back into the ad platforms, which is what ClearSignal does.

Do you lock us into a contract?

No. We work month-to-month and keep 95%+ of our clients because the numbers justify it.

Can you fix our HubSpot?

Yes. Our HubSpot-certified team handles portal cleanup, lifecycle stages, pipelines, workflows, lead routing and reporting.

Will this replace our CRM or intake software?

No. ClearSignal sits on top of the CRM you already use, with native two-way sync for HubSpot, Salesforce and Lead Docket, and custom connections for most tools with API access.

Will you share our results with other firms?

Never by name. We highlight anonymized wins only, and we never display client names or logos without written permission.
