🔁
Standout Feature+
Offline conversion sync — ClearSignal sends signed-client outcomes back to Google and Meta automatically, training their bidding algorithms to find more clients like your best ones, not just more cheap leads.
What It Means
Google and Meta normally only see a "lead" or "call" event — they have no idea which of those ever became a paying client. ClearSignal connects to their offline conversion / Conversions API and automatically uploads the real outcome from your CRM, tied back to the original click.
What Problem It Solves
Without this, your ad platforms keep optimizing for whatever's cheap to generate, even if those leads rarely turn into signed clients. You end up paying to attract more of exactly the wrong kind of lead.
Why It's Important
This is the one lever that compounds — it keeps improving every month as more signed-client data flows back in, rather than a one-time fix that plateaus.

Common Questions

Before You Book a Call

Do you only work with law firms?+
No. AMP was built first inside real law firm marketing operations — estate planning and personal injury specifically — but the underlying system (PPC + attribution + CRM automation + CMO strategy) applies to any client-based professional service: financial advisors, healthcare practices, home services, and beyond.
Do I have to buy all four pillars, or can I start with one?+
Start wherever your firm actually needs help right now. Most firms see the biggest compounding effect running everything together, but PPC Management, ClearSignal attribution, CRM automation, and Fractional CMO strategy can each be scoped and run independently.
Does ClearSignal replace my existing CRM?+
No. ClearSignal connects to the CRM you already use — HubSpot, Salesforce, and others — rather than asking you to migrate your data somewhere new. It's the attribution and reporting layer sitting on top of what you have.
How is this different from a generic marketing agency?+
Most agencies report on impressions and clicks. AMP is built to report on — and optimize toward — paying clients, using attribution data pulled directly from your own CRM. Strategy is also led directly by the founder, not handed off to a junior account manager.
How long does setup actually take?+
It depends on how many pillars and integrations your firm needs, but most firms are fully live within 2-3 weeks of the onboarding meeting. Ad accounts can typically launch faster; attribution and CRM automation take slightly longer to wire up correctly.
What's the "AMP Axis" you keep mentioning?+
It's the core principle behind how AMP is built: the more of the connected system you run — ads, attribution, CRM automation, and CMO strategy — the more each piece compounds the others. Running one in isolation gets you a linear improvement. Running all four together gets you a compounding one.
What happens on the strategy call?+
A real diagnostic, not a sales pitch — we'll look at your current ad accounts, CRM, and reporting setup, show you exactly where the gaps and costs are, and give you a straight answer on scope and pricing. You leave with clarity either way.
