The manual-work problem

Your team is retyping what clients already told you.

Every clipboard, PDF form and sticky note becomes a data entry task. It slows intake, introduces errors and burns hours your staff could spend with clients.

Re-keying everything

Staff copy intake answers into the CRM, then again into documents. Hours a week, plus typos.

Slow, clunky client experience

Clients wait on paperwork and repeat the same details to three different people.

Data that never connects

Sources, notes and intake details stay on paper, so reporting and attribution break.

How it works

Capture once. Use everywhere.

1. Clients check in and complete intake

On a lobby tablet or their own phone. Branded to your firm, with the questions your practice needs.

2. Data lands in your CRM automatically

Contact details, case facts and lead source sync to HubSpot, Salesforce or Lead Docket instantly.

3. Documents start pre-filled

Captured details populate intake documents and drafts, so staff review and finish instead of starting from scratch.

4. The right person is notified

Front desk, attorney or intake rep gets an alert the moment a client arrives or submits.

5. Admins see everything

Check-in volume, wait times and completion rates in one view, connected to ClearSignal.

6. Consistent, secure records

Every client follows the same process, with the same fields, every time.

Who it helps

Better for everyone who touches intake.

Your clients

Your team

No more data entry or retyping. Faster drafting, fewer errors and more time for the work that actually needs a person.

Admins and partners

Streamlined processes, consistent data and visibility into intake, without chasing paperwork.
