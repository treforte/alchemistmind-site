What's inside

Everything between the click and the signed retainer.

Multi-channel attribution

Google Ads, LSA, Meta, Reddit, CallRail and organic, matched to CRM outcomes at the lead level.

Offline conversion sync

Signed-case outcomes sent to Google and Meta conversion APIs automatically, so bidding targets clients.

Native CRM sync

Two-way sync with HubSpot, Salesforce and Lead Docket. Custom connections for most tools with API access.

Automated reporting

Cost per lead, cost per qualified lead and cost per signed case by channel, campaign and practice area.

Local intelligence

Local rankings, brand monitoring and competitor visibility in the same view as your paid data.

Practice-specific modules

Hospital lien tracking for PI, consult-to-retainer tracking for estate planning, and custom journey mapping.

The feedback loop

Most firms train Google to find more leads. ClearSignal trains it to find more clients.

01

A lead or call comes in

ClearSignal captures the click ID, source and campaign.

02

The case signs (or doesn't)

Your CRM records the outcome. ClearSignal matches it automatically.

03

Outcomes go back to the platforms

Signed cases are sent to Google and Meta via offline conversion APIs.

04

The algorithm finds more like them

Bidding shifts toward people who look like your best clients. CAC drops over time.

FAQ

ClearSignal questions

Does it replace my CRM?

No. ClearSignal sits on top of your existing CRM and reads outcomes from it.

How long does setup take?

Most firms are live in about two weeks. Timing depends mainly on how quickly we get account access.

How is client data secured?

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and access is scoped per firm. We'll review security details with your team on the call.

Can I try it before I commit?

ClearSignal is demo-only because we demo on live accounts, not sandboxes. The Check-In App has a 14-day free trial.

Is it only for PI and estate planning?

No. It works for any consultation-based practice. We map your terminology and client journey during onboarding.

Do I need Alchemist Mind Pros to run my ads to use it?

No. ClearSignal works with in-house teams and other agencies too.
