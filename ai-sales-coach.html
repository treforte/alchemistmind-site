The problem

You're paying for leads that die on the intake call.

A bad marketing month and a bad intake month look identical in the numbers. Without call review, you can't tell which one you have, and nobody has time to listen to every call.

Calls nobody reviews

Managers spot-check a handful of calls a month, if that. Patterns go unnoticed for quarters.

Inconsistent reps

Your best closer signs half her consults. Your newest rep signs one in five. Nobody knows exactly why.

Expensive leads, cheap follow-through

At $300+ per lead, every fumbled objection is real money walking out the door.

How it helps

Like game film for your intake team.

Great teams review film after every game. AI Sales Coach does that for every consult, automatically, so coaching is based on what actually happened on the call.

  • Automatic scoring

    Each call scored on rapport, discovery, objection handling and close using a fixed legal-intake rubric.

  • Plain-language coaching notes

    Specific, timestamped feedback reps can apply on the next call.

  • Objection playbook

    The objections your callers raise most, with the responses that are working for your best closers.

  • Coaching emails after every call

    Reps get their score and specific next-step tips automatically, no manager required.

  • Leaderboard and goals

    Rep rankings and goal tracking so managers know who needs help and who to learn from.

What do I need to submit?

A call recording or transcript. It works with most call recording and tracking tools you already use.

Is this a generic AI wrapper?

No. Scoring uses a fixed rubric built for legal intake, with a human review loop to keep feedback accurate.

Can I customize the rubric?

Yes. We map scoring to your intake process and terminology during onboarding.

Will my reps feel micromanaged?

The best teams use it as coaching, not surveillance. Reps get specific notes they can use on the next call, and wins are celebrated on the leaderboard.
