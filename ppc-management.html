Why most law firm PPC underperforms

The ads aren't the problem. The scoreboard is.

If your campaigns optimize for form fills, the platform will happily deliver cheap, unqualified ones. We fix the scoreboard first, then the campaigns.

Wrong conversion goals

Page views, button clicks and 10-second calls counted as conversions, teaching Google to chase junk.

Search term leakage

Broad match and loose PMax spending on job seekers, free-advice hunters and the wrong practice areas.

No outcome feedback

Signed cases never make it back to the ad platforms, so bidding never improves where it counts.

How much do you charge for PPC management?

Pricing is based on spend and scope. We'll give you an exact number after the audit call. Every engagement is month-to-month and backed by our 45-day results guarantee.

Do you need access to our CRM?

To optimize on signed cases, yes. We connect read access to outcomes through ClearSignal. Without it, we're limited to lead-level optimization like everyone else.

Do you follow legal advertising rules?

Yes. We build ads and landing pages with state bar advertising rules in mind and work with your team on any required disclaimers.

Can you work alongside our in-house team?

Yes. Some firms hand us everything; others keep a channel in-house and use us for Google Ads and ClearSignal.
